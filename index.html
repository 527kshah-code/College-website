About GT and Our Mission

At the Georgia Institute of Technology, we are driven by a singular purpose: to develop leaders who advance technology and improve the human condition.

Founded in 1885 in the heart of Atlanta, Georgia Tech is a top-tier public research university dedicated to inclusive innovation. Guided by our motto, "Progress and Service," we break down barriers to make education and technology broadly accessible. From our world-class labs to global communities, our faculty, staff, and students work together to deliver real-world solutions that serve the public good.
